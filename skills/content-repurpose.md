# Content repurpose

**Use when:** "turn this into posts", "what else can I do with this".

One long piece — a podcast episode, a talk, a long email — becomes a week of
material.

## How to pull it apart

1. Find the **three or four real ideas** in it. Not topics. Ideas someone could
   disagree with.
2. For each one, the strongest **hook** is a line the author already said. Use
   their words before inventing your own.
3. Build outward from each idea:
   - A short post: the hook, the idea, one concrete example, a next step.
   - An email: same idea, more room, written like one person to one person.
   - A clip suggestion: the timestamp or the quote worth cutting.

## The rules that carry

- **The hook is the headline, and the headline is the whole game.** If it does
  not stop someone, the rest was never read.
- **Sell the feeling, not the thing.**
- **Every piece needs a next step.** Content with nowhere to go is attention you
  got and then wasted.
- **Plain beats polished.** Write like a person talking, not a brand.
- **Never invent a fact, a number, or a story** that was not in the source. If a
  claim needs a number you do not have, cut the claim.
