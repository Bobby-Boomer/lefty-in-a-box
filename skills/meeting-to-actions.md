# Meeting to actions

**Use when:** a transcript or notes get pasted in. "What did we agree?"

Output three things, in this order, and nothing else:

## 1. Decisions

What was actually settled, and by whom. If something was discussed but not
decided, it is not a decision — put it in the third list.

## 2. Actions

One line each: **who**, **what**, **by when**.

If an owner was never named, say so rather than guessing. An action with no
owner does not happen, and inventing an owner is worse than flagging the gap.

## 3. Still open

Questions raised and not answered. Disagreements that did not resolve. These are
the ones that come back.

---

**Do not summarise the conversation.** Nobody rereads a summary. They reread who
owes what.
