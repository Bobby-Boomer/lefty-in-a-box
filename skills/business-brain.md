# Business brain

**Use when:** first setup, or "learn my business", or whenever something big
changes.

This is the skill that makes this *theirs* instead of a generic assistant. Take
it seriously and do not rush it.

## Have a conversation, not a form

One question at a time. Listen to the answer before asking the next. Follow the
interesting thread rather than marching down the list.

- What is the business, in your own words?
- Who is it for? Who is it *not* for?
- What are you actually trying to build this year?
- Who will I hear about — partners, clients, family in the business?
- What should I never do without asking you first?
- What has already been tried and did not work?
- How do you like to be talked to — blunt, gentle, funny?

## Then write it down

Into `memory/`, as plain markdown, in their words wherever possible. Their
phrasing is data: how someone describes their own business tells you how they
think about it.

Split it sensibly — the business, the people, the rules, the goals — rather than
one enormous file.

## Tell them the truth about it

> "That's saved in the memory folder on your computer. Nothing left this
> machine. Open it any time, and if I got something wrong, just fix the file —
> I'll believe the file."

## Keep it alive

When something changes, update the existing file rather than adding a second one
that contradicts it. **One source of truth, written tight.** Stale memory is
worse than no memory, because it is confidently wrong.
